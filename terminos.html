Terms of Service

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. What this service is

“Canal Origen Uploader” is a private tool run by Canal Origen, a channel that makes short educational videos about the origin of everyday products (published as Canal Origen in Spanish, Origin Channel in English and Canal Origem in Portuguese). The tool sends videos made by Canal Origen to Canal Origen's own accounts on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube. It is not offered to the public, and only accounts owned by Canal Origen are connected to it.

2. How it works with each platform

  • TikTok: after the account owner authorizes it, the tool uploads each video to the TikTok drafts inbox. The account owner reviews it in the TikTok app and decides whether to publish it.
  • Instagram: the tool publishes Reels to Canal Origen's professional account at the time the owner has approved.
  • YouTube: the tool uploads Shorts as private and schedules them to go public at the approved time.

The account owner can withdraw this access at any time from the settings of each platform.

3. Content

All videos are produced by Canal Origen. Every video is reviewed and approved by a person before it is sent. Images come from Wikimedia Commons and are credited under their licenses; music is by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com) under CC BY 4.0. The tool must only be used to upload content that follows the rules of each platform.

4. Platform terms

Use of TikTok, Instagram and YouTube through this tool is also subject to the TikTok Terms of Service, the Meta and Instagram Terms of Use and the YouTube Terms of Service.

5. No warranty

The tool is provided as is, for Canal Origen's own use. Canal Origen does not guarantee that it will always be available or free of errors, and is not responsible for changes or outages of the platforms it connects to.
